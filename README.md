<p align="center"><i>A signal cuts through the static. You are the operator on the other end of a CRT terminal - decode it, descend through the grid, and find out what's broadcasting.</i></p>

<p align="center"><b>Created for Ludum Dare</b></p>

<b>what's-the-signal</b> is a pixel-art auto-battler roguelike rendered entirely on a green-phosphor monitor. Build a loadout, scout enemies on the radar, and survive long enough to triangulate the source.

<h2>HOW TO PLAY</h2>

<ul>
<li><b>TAB</b> - open the radar / map</li>
<li><b>WASD</b> - move on the grid</li>
<li><b>Combat resolves automatically</b> - your job is positioning and the loadout you brought in</li>
<li><b>Pause / fast-forward</b> with the speed controls (top of the screen)</li>
</ul>

<h2>FEATURES</h2>

<ul>
<li><b>Auto-battler combat</b> - position your loadout, watch encounters resolve in real time.</li>
<li><b>Dozens of artifacts across multiple types and tiers</b> — every run is a different stat curve.</li>
<li><b>Radar exploration</b> - discover chests, enemies, and terrain on a tile grid that fogs in as you move.</li>
<li><b>Treasure chest choices</b> - pick one, skip for coins, regret nothing.</li>
<li><b>CRT phosphor aesthetic</b> - an 8-color green palette, scanlines, pixel sigils. A single accent red, used only for damage and bosses.</li>
<li><b>One-handed play</b> - runs in your browser, no install.</li>
</ul>

<h2>TREASURE CHESTS</h2>

Every chest is a fork: take one of the artifacts on offer, or skip the lot for coins. Coin runs are valid runs.

<h2>ENEMIES</h2>

Several enemy archetypes (E01, E02, E03, …) with distinct movement, range, and signal patterns. Read the codex; a misread costs HP.
